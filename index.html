Our Story

A little corner of Portugal on Earls' Street.

O Cabaz — “the basket” — is a family-run restaurant, bakery and café in the heart of Thetford. We bake fresh every morning, pull proper Portuguese espresso, and grill the plates we grew up with.

Fresh Coffee & Bakery

Portuguese-roasted espresso, pastéis de nata and cakes baked in-house every single morning.

Dine-In or Takeaway

Settle into our cosy dining room, or grab your order to go — packed fresh and ready when you are.

Portuguese Beers & Wines

Cold Super Bock, crisp Vinho Verde, Douro reds and a proper glass of port to finish.

Friendly Local Atmosphere

Family-run, unpretentious and genuinely warm. Regulars are made here, not born.
